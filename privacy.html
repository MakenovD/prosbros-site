Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

ProsBros ("we", "us") is a service of Flowsell, Inc. It helps home-service professionals ("pros") answer and follow up with their customers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account info from pros: name, business name, email, phone, billing details (processed by Stripe; we never store full card numbers).
  • Thumbtack data you connect: when a pro connects Thumbtack through Thumbtack's official sign-in (OAuth), we receive the leads, messages, business profile and reviews that the pro authorizes. We never receive or store Thumbtack passwords.
  • Customer contact info: names, messages and, where provided, phone numbers of the pro's customers, used only to communicate on that pro's behalf.
  • Website analytics: we use PostHog to understand how visitors use prosbros.app (pages viewed, clicks, device and approximate location). Waitlist form entries are stored there too.

How we use it

  • To reply to and follow up with a pro's customers in that pro's name, using only the services and prices the pro sets.
  • To alert the pro when a customer is ready to book, and to show results in the pro's dashboard.
  • To run, secure and improve the service, and to contact pros about their account.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use one pro's customer data for any other pro or for our own marketing.

Text messages (SMS)

We send text messages only to people who agreed to receive them. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Who we share data with

Service providers that help us run ProsBros — hosting, AI model providers that draft replies, messaging providers (such as Twilio), payments (Stripe) and analytics (PostHog) — under contracts that limit their use of the data. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while an account is active. When a pro disconnects Thumbtack or closes the account, we delete the related customer data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it. Pros can disconnect ProsBros at any time from their Thumbtack account or from ours.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act. Email hello@prosbros.app and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access tokens are stored encrypted, and access is limited to people who need it to run the service.

Children

ProsBros is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the date above. Questions: hello@prosbros.app.
