Straight answers

Can this get my Thumbtack account in trouble?

You sign in on Thumbtack's own page and can disconnect us anytime. We never see your password and don't use bots — just Thumbtack's official connection for partner apps (approval in progress).

Will it quote a price I don't charge?

No. It only quotes the rates you give it. Custom work, discounts, scheduling — that comes to you.

Will you text my customers?

Only ones who said OK to texts, from a number in your business name. Every text says who it's from, and STOP works right away.

What if I want to answer myself?

Just answer. It backs off that customer the second you reply.

How long to get set up?

About 10 minutes, on your own: connect Thumbtack, check your rates, OK the first batch. Want a hand? Book a free 15-minute call.

Can I quit?

Anytime. No contract, no setup fee.
