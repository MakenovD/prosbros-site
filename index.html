Straight answers

What about my old Thumbtack leads?

Thumbtack's partner connection only shows leads that come in after you connect, so Bro starts with your next one. Past customers whose numbers you have can get a text later — only if they agreed to texts.

Will it quote a price I don't charge?

No. It only quotes the rates you give it. Custom work, discounts, scheduling — that comes to you.

Will you text my customers?

Only ones who said OK to texts, from a number in your business name. Every text says who it's from, and STOP works right away.

What if I want to answer myself?

Just answer. It backs off that customer the second you reply.

How long to get set up?

Once your pilot spot opens, about 10 minutes: connect Thumbtack, check your rates, and Bro takes your next lead. Want a hand? Book a free 15-minute call.

Can I quit?

Anytime. No contract, no setup fee.

Is this AI?

Yes — Bro is software set up with your prices and rules. It writes in your name, and anything it isn't sure about comes to you.

How do I know it's working?

You see messages sent, answers, jobs booked and dollars in. Log a job amount with one tap.
